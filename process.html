Process(03) / Process

Built piece by deliberate piece.

Four stagesTwo to twelve weeksYou see it the whole way

Every site is assembled the way a machine is: parts chosen on purpose, seated properly, tested under load. Nothing gets glued on at the end because we ran out of time.

(01)Stages

From the bench to launch day.

Stage 01

The bench

Before anything is designed, we lay out what you sell, who is buying, and what has to happen on the page. We look at your current site, your competitors and where enquiries come from today.

You leave with a written scope, a fixed price and a date.

Typically one week
Stage 02

The core

Structure and design together: layout, type, the path a visitor takes and the one thing you want them to do. Real pages with your own words, in a browser, on a phone.

Two rounds of revisions are built into the price.

One to two weeks
Stage 03

The build

Hand-written markup, fast hosting, real accessibility. A live preview link on day one of this stage that updates as we go, so there is no big reveal and no surprise.

Content, images and copy are finalised here.

One to six weeks
Stage 04

The launch

Domain connected, email verified as still arriving, analytics wired, and every form submitted for real from a phone before we call it live.

Then hosting, updates, backups and monitoring for as long as you want us.

Usually an hour
A site is not finished at launch. It is just lit.

Which is why we stay on it after

(02)Your part

What we need from you.

Projects slip for one reason more than any other, and it is never the code. Here is the honest list.

01

One decision maker

Someone who can say yes. Design by committee doubles the timeline and rarely improves the result.

02

Your content, early

Copy, photos, logos, licence numbers. We can write and source, but we cannot invent what only you know.

03

Access

Domain registrar, current host, analytics. We will walk you through finding each one if you have lost them.

04

Feedback in rounds

Collected and sent together rather than trickling in over a fortnight. It gets you a better site faster.

05

Honesty about the deadline

If there is a real date, say so at the bench. We will tell you whether it is possible before you pay anything.

06

Patience with the boring part

Migrations, DNS and email are the least interesting and the highest risk. They get done carefully.

(03)Timelines

How long it really takes.

These are the ranges we actually hit, not best cases. The date you get at the bench is the one we hold.

  1. 2–3 weeks

    A focused local site

    Up to five pages, a contact form tested for real, the search basics.

  2. 4–6 weeks

    Most business sites

    Custom design throughout, integrations, content migrated from an old site.

  3. 8–12 weeks

    Commerce, booking, apps

    E-commerce, booking systems, or an application with accounts and a workflow behind it.
