# Revenue

Written weekly by the revenue Routine from QuickBooks. Do not hand-edit
below the line — edits are overwritten on the next sweep.

## What the sweep records

- MRR from active recurring invoices
- AR aging: who owes what, and for how long
- Overdue invoices, oldest first
- Payment links issued and not yet paid

## Why it matters here rather than in QuickBooks

QuickBooks knows the numbers but not the client context. Sitting next to the
client notes, an overdue invoice can be read against what was shipped that
month and whether a report ever went out. An unpaid retainer whose last
report is blank is usually not a billing problem.
