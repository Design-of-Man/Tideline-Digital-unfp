The Studio

Small on purpose,
careful by default.

Design of Man is a small studio. The person who scopes your project is the person who designs it, builds it, and answers the phone after launch.

Why we're small

No account layer between you and the build.

Most agencies grow by adding people between the client and the work. We went the other way. Fewer projects at a time, each one handled start to finish by the people actually doing it.

That means you get straight answers about timelines, honest pushback when an idea will not serve you, and a site nobody has to reverse-engineer six months later.

We write our own markup. We do not stack plugins until something works. When a page is slow or an element is unreachable by keyboard, that is a defect we fix, not a trade-off we ask you to accept.

We build the foundation your business stands on, not a template stretched to fit.
measured, drafted, and built for the long haul
