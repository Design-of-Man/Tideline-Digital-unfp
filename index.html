What we actually do.

Design of Man is a two-person studio that designs, builds, and then keeps running websites and web apps. Not a marketing agency that also touches code, and not a freelancer who disappears after launch — the same people who scope your project are the ones who build it and the ones who answer when something needs fixing.

Everything you would ask on the call.

How much does a custom website cost?

It depends on what the site has to do. A brochure site for a local business and a custom build with booking, payments and integrations are very different jobs. The consult ends with an exact number for your scope.

How long does a project take?

Simple sites launch in two to three weeks. Most business sites run four to six weeks from kickoff, and larger custom or e-commerce builds take eight to twelve. You get the schedule before we start.

Will my site work on mobile?

Always. Every site we build is responsive and tested across phones, tablets and desktops. Mobile is part of the process, not an afterthought.

Do you handle hosting and updates after launch?

Yes. Care plans cover managed hosting, security updates, backups, uptime monitoring and content updates, priced to the site they look after.

Can you redesign or migrate my existing site?

Absolutely. We regularly rebuild aging sites and migrate content, domains and email from your old host with no downtime. We audit what you have and tell you honestly what is worth keeping.
