Why the numbers are small.

Because they are measured rather than rounded up. The figures come from Google Search Console for the 32 days after launch against the 32 before, compared weekday to weekday so a weekend does not flatter the result.

The enquiry count is the site’s own form; the previous site had no lead capture, so there is no percentage to quote. More of the work is under agreements that do not let us publish it. A studio two years old claiming forty case studies is counting something other than clients.
